Endings and arrivals

The first pass gave losing a beat, the Leviathan a horn, and its parts a freeze. These four cover what it left: winning, ramming, the way enemies turn up, and the hit that lands twice. Each one names the number to turn if it’s wrong.

17

Let the Leviathan die on screen

Yes3 hyou judge the length
Today

The killing hit sends the boss back to its pool on that frame. Its death sound plays, the Leviathan Down card goes up, and time stops. After a ten-minute run, the thing you fought never visibly dies. Item 8 gave losing a beat; winning still has none.

Change

About 2.5 seconds between the kill and the card:

  • A 0.15 s freeze on the killing hit.
  • Time drops to a quarter while explosions run along the hull, from the emplacements outward.
  • The rounds still in the air go out, and one large blast takes the hull.
  • Then the card comes up as it does now.

It runs on the death beat’s machinery, so nothing can hurt the ship or pause the game while it plays.

The dial

The 2.5 seconds.

18

A ship you ram goes up

Yes30 minme
Today

Flying into an enemy costs you a hit point, and the enemy is taken off the ring on the spot, with no explosion and no death sound. It looks as if it passed through you and vanished.

Change

It explodes, with its own death sound. Ramming still earns nothing and doesn’t count as a kill; that part is deliberate and stays.

19

Let enemies arrive rather than appear

Yes2–3 hyou judge the look
Today

Each enemy is put down at full size on the frame it spawns, on a random bearing anywhere round the ring. Nothing keeps a spawn away from your ship.

Measured

Today, with the bot flying for two and a half minutes: 57 of 85 enemies were already in the camera’s view on the frame they spawned. Most were across the arena, but 4 appeared within 8 units of the ship, one of them 3.9 units away.

Change

A short arrival, about 0.4 s: a flash, and the ship grows in from nothing. It can’t hurt you or be hit until it’s in. Spawns also skip the stretch of ring about 30° either side of your ship, so nothing arrives on top of you. How many enemies come, and when, doesn’t change.

The dials

The 0.4 s, and the 30°.

20

Give the ship a moment’s grace after a hit

Yes1.5 hyou judge the length
Today

Apart from the dash and the Deflector, nothing protects the ship. Straight after a hit, the next round lands as hard as the first, so two rounds from one volley can take two hit points in the same instant.

Measured

Across the bot’s 50 logged runs, 701 hits: 61 of them took more than one hit point at once, and 85 more landed within a quarter of a second of the hit before. The bot dodges worse than you do, so you’ll meet this less often, but the volleys are the same.

Change

After a hit, the ship blinks and can’t be hurt for half a second. It makes the game a little easier, which is why it’s your call and not a fix. The dash and the Deflector stay as they are.

The dial

The half second.

Menus, loads and the end card

Nothing here changes how a run plays. It covers the moments around a run: pausing, dying, restarting, loading, and the settings.

21

Put Restart on the pause screen, and check before abandoning a run

Yes2 hme
Today

The pause screen has Resume, Options, Main Menu and Quit. One press of Main Menu or Quit ends a run of up to ten minutes, and Quit closes the game outright. There’s no Restart, so starting over means dying or going through the title screen.

Change

Restart goes between Options and Main Menu. Restart, Main Menu and Quit each ask “Abandon this run?”, with No selected. The death and victory cards don’t ask, because the run is already over there.

22

Stop a dash press from skipping the end card

Yes45 minme
Today

On a pad, A is dash, and A also presses whichever menu button is selected. Both end cards open with Try Again selected. So an A pressed just as the card comes up, which is the reflex when a hit is coming, starts a new run before you’ve read the card, and a NEW BEST goes with it.

Change

For the first 0.6 s the card’s buttons fade in and ignore presses.

23

Fade the music across a load

Yes1 hyou listen
Today

The title music plays on behind the loading screen until the arena is ready, then stops dead, and the run’s track starts at full volume on the same frame. The same happens in the other direction.

Change

The old track fades out as the loading screen comes up, and the new one fades in over about a second once the run is on screen.

24

Pause when the pad is unplugged

Yes45 minme
Today

The game doesn’t notice a pad going away. If the cable comes out or the battery dies mid-run, the ship stops answering while the waves keep coming.

Change

If the pad you were using disconnects, the run pauses, as it already does when the window loses focus, and the pause screen says “Controller disconnected”. When the pad comes back, the game stays paused until you resume.

25

Show the run so far on the pause screen

Yes1 hme
Today

Only the end card shows the run’s numbers: time, level, kills, and how often you took each skill. During a run, the only way to see your build is to remember it, and the build is what each offer asks you to think about.

Change

The pause screen shows the same summary under its buttons. The end card’s code writes it, so the two can’t disagree.

26

Say what ended the run

Yes2–3 hme
Today

Ship Lost says how long you lasted, not what got you. The game doesn’t know either: a hit arrives with no source attached. That’s also why every playtest-bot report says “damage cause unknown”.

Change

Each hit carries its source: a Fighter’s round, a Heavy’s, a torpedo, the lance, the curtain, ramming a Scout, the Leviathan’s hull. The card’s first line becomes “Lost to a Leviathan torpedo”. The bot logs the source of every hit, so the next balance question comes with data.

27

A tip on the loading screen

Later1 hyou read the wording
Today

The hints teach the buttons, not the rules, and some rules aren’t stated anywhere:

  • the dash passes through rounds untouched;
  • ramming costs a hit point and earns nothing;
  • salvage drops more often the more damaged you are;
  • the Leviathan’s hull can’t be hurt while its emplacements stand.
Change

One line under the loading bar, from about ten, never the same twice in a row.

Why later

The loading screen is up for about a second and a half, which is short for reading. It pays off once someone new is playing.

28

Hear the level as you set it

Yes30 minme
Today

Moving Master or Effects in Options makes no sound, so on the title or pause screen you set a level you can’t hear. Music is the exception, because its track is playing (muffled under pause since item 14).

Change

Each step of Master or Effects plays a short sound at the new level, no more than one every tenth of a second, so holding the stick doesn’t make a buzz.

31

A screenshot key

Yes45 minme
Today

The game can’t save a screenshot. I take mine inside the editor, because I don’t capture your desktop, so I’ve never seen a frame from a real build. Yours come through Windows’ own tools, and they don’t record which build they came from.

Change

F12 saves the frame, HUD included, at full resolution to Pictures\Survival Chaos. The file name carries the build stamp and the time, for example 26_0930-0825_polish 8ee1a3a+ 2026-09-30 21.14.07.png. A “Saved” note shows for a second, and it isn’t in the picture. F12 joins the Controls tab.

32

Look for first-time stutters

Yes, measure30 min, and 2–4 h if foundyour run carries it
Today

DirectX 12 has to build a draw state the first time something is drawn. Measured in a build on 29 September, that makes the arena’s first two frames take 0.48 and 0.28 s. The loading screen stays up through them for exactly that reason. Nothing covers the Leviathan’s first frames at ten minutes, or its attacks’ first frames after that. No stutter has been seen yet, which is why this starts as a measurement.

Change

The next build logs any frame over 50 ms along with what happened on it, so your run finds out. If the Leviathan’s arrival hitches, Unity 6 can record the draw states in one run and build them behind the loading screen.

How it would ship

  1. One build with everything you say yes to. Last time that worked, because each item named what to watch. The open list’s start-here card gets one line per item. For any you don’t flag, the answer on it stands.
  2. Before that build, two things from you: an icon from three candidates I’ll send (29), whether the emails stay (30), and nothing else unless a dial feels wrong.
  3. Your run. It needs to reach the Leviathan once, for 17 and 32. The debug menu’s Skip to boss is fine for that.

Left out, and why

  • A sound on every hit that doesn’t kill. On 28 September you found the gun loud and repetitive, and this would add to that same stream of sound.
  • Notches on the Leviathan’s bar, one per act. They’d put the fight’s structure on screen, which is close to the boss HUD you reverted on 8 September.
  • A shape for salvage, not just a colour. The colour check (7) found salvage against the lava to be the weakest pair (ΔE 16, and 9 under protanopia), but the two didn’t merge. Worth doing only if you lose a piece in a run.
  • Everything the first roadmap left out: screen shake, phase names on screen, the fireflies, FSR 3 frame generation, and trails behind the rounds. Key rebinding (16) stays parked.

New enemies, new skills, a second boss, difficulty settings and an endless mode are still content, not polish. I can write a content roadmap if you want one.
