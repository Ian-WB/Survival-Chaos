The Moon
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The moon in the sky is painted from a map of the real one: the colour map
in the CGI Moon Kit of NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio, made from
Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera data. It is in the public domain.
NASA asks to be credited where it can be, so here it is. NASA does not
endorse this game.

  https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720
